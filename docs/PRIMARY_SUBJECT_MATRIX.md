# Primary subject catalogue (KSSR Semakan) — NOT complete subject content

Applies to Tahun 1–6, with SK/SJKC/SJKT and optional SK language offerings.
The UI selects appropriate **subject names** by year and stream, without claiming that unreviewed lesson content is available.

## Core year 1–3
Bahasa Melayu; Bahasa Inggeris; Matematik; Sains; Pendidikan Islam or Pendidikan Moral (depending on pupil enrolment); Pendidikan Jasmani dan Pendidikan Kesihatan; Pendidikan Kesenian. SJKC additionally Bahasa Cina, SJKT additionally Bahasa Tamil.

## Core year 4–6
All applicable core language, Mathematics, Science and religious/moral/physical education subjects plus Sejarah, Reka Bentuk dan Teknologi, Pendidikan Seni Visual, Pendidikan Muzik. SJKC additionally Bahasa Cina, SJKT additionally Bahasa Tamil.

## Optional SK offerings
Bahasa Arab, Bahasa Cina SK, Bahasa Tamil SK, Bahasa Iban, Bahasa Kadazandusun, Bahasa Semai. Availability is decided by the school, not assumed from the student's account.

## Important
1. The DSKP for each school type and year must be validated before approving lesson content.
2. The original draft year-one Mathematics materials are SK-first, not official SJKC/SJKT translations.
3. As of this release, new Year 1 mathematics material is **DRAFT_TEACHER_REVIEW**; other subjects are catalogue entries only.
4. Some documents organise lower-primary arts under Pendidikan Kesenian and upper-primary arts under separate visual arts and music; retain version metadata for school-year variants.
5. Future primary subjects and optional offerings should be configured in Sheets after DSKP verification.
