# Virtual Science Lab sandbox

The first sandbox has three original qualitative models: established plant growth, density-based floating/sinking of solid objects, and an ideal battery/bulb circuit. Each offers a question, prediction, variable controls, observed result, reset and a 12-entry temporary notebook. No external APIs, real equipment, personal-data collection or certified assessment. Each model explicitly states its assumptions and limits.

Next review stages: educator validation, accessible diagrams, repeated trials with controlled measurements, then additional light/shadow, magnetism, evaporation and simple-machine experiments. Do not claim validated scientific measurements or complete syllabus coverage.
